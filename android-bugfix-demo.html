Bug 2

Back button jumps straight to Home

From almost anywhere, pressing back (top-bar arrow or the Android back gesture) skips the previous screen and lands on Home. Try it: open Flashcards → a deck → back, or switch tabs and press back.

During the Android back animation a white line/strip appears on the right edge and follows the transition until it completes. It shows because a gap opens between the two sliding screens and something white sits behind them.
